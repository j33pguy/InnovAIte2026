# MFA Setup & Password Change Guide

Follow these steps to set up your workshop account before accessing Microsoft Fabric.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- Your **temporary credentials** (username and password) from the sheet included in your workshop packet
- A **smartphone** (iOS or Android)
- The **Microsoft Authenticator** app installed on your phone
  - [Download for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/microsoft-authenticator/id983156458)
  - [Download for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.azure.authenticator)

---

## Step 1: Sign In & Change Your Password

1. Open a browser and go to [portal.microsoft.com](https://portal.microsoft.com).
2. Enter the **username** from your credentials email and click **Next**.
3. Enter the **temporary password** and click **Sign in**.
4. You will be prompted to **update your password**. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Current password** — enter the temporary password again
   - **New password** — choose a strong password (at least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols)
   - **Confirm password** — re-enter your new password
5. Click **Sign in**.

> **Tip:** Write down or save your new password somewhere safe — you will need it for the rest of the workshop.

---

## Step 2: Set Up MFA with Microsoft Authenticator

After changing your password, you will see a **"More information required"** prompt. This is the MFA enrollment screen.

1. Click **Next** on the "More information required" page.
2. On the **"Keep your account secure"** page, select **Microsoft Authenticator** and click **Next**.
3. If prompted to install the app, click **Next** (you should already have it installed from the prerequisites).
4. You will see a page that says **"Set up your account"**. On your phone:
   - Open the **Microsoft Authenticator** app.
   - Tap the **+** button in the top right corner.
   - Select **Work or school account**.
   - Select **Scan a QR code**.
5. **Scan the QR code** displayed on your computer screen with your phone's camera.
6. Once the account is added, click **Next** on the computer.
7. A **test notification** will be sent to your phone. On your phone:
   - You will see a notification from Microsoft Authenticator.
   - Open the notification and tap **Approve**.
   - If prompted, enter the **number shown on your computer screen** into the app.
8. Once approved, click **Next** on the computer.
9. Click **Done** to complete MFA setup.
